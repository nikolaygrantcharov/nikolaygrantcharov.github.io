Seminars and Conferences

BunG Seminar

In January 2023, I co-founded the University of Chicago BunG Seminar with Aaron Slipper and subsequently organized it for seven consecutive quarters. The seminar was devoted to the study of the geometry and representation theory of the moduli stack of principal bundles on a curve. It provided background for UChicago's Geometric Langlands Seminar and featured talks by many experts in the field, including Alexander Beilinson, Ngô Bảo Châu, Victor Ginzburg, Nick Rozenblyum, and Zhiwei Yun.
