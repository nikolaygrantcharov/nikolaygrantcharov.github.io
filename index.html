Nikolay Grantcharov

Limited-Term Assistant Professor of Mathematics
University of Georgia

I am a geometric representation theorist currently working at the University of Georgia. My research interests include Higgs and Coulomb branches, symplectic daulity, DAHAs, deformation theory, and algebraic geometry.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Chicago, where I was supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and advised by Victor Ginzburg. Before that, I earned my Bachelor's degree in mathematics from the University of California, Berkeley.

Announcements

Deformation theory of the \((C_n^\vee,C_n)\)-DAHA

Preprint, 2026.

We provide a zeros-and-residues realizations and formal deformations of the double affine Hecke algebra associated with the non-reduced affine root system of type \((C_n^\vee,C_n)\). arXiv

Quasi-classical Braverman-–Kazhdan intertwiners via quiver varieties

Preprint, 2026.

We construct isomorphisms between affinized cotangent bundles of Braverman–Kazhdan spaces in type A using reflection functors for quiver varieties with an SL-gauge group. arXiv
