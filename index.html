Nikolay Grantcharov

Limited-Term Assistant Professor of Mathematics
University of Georgia

I am a mathematician working in geometric representation theory. My research interests include symplectic geometry, double affine Hecke algebras, quiver varieties, deformation theory, and algebraic geometry.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Chicago, where my advisor was Victor Ginzburg.

Recent work

Deformation theory of the CCn∨-DAHA

Preprint, 2026.

We study zeros-and-residues realizations and formal deformations of the double affine Hecke algebra associated with the non-reduced affine root system of type CCn∨.

Symmetries of Braverman–Kazhdan spaces via quiver varieties

Preprint, 2026.

We construct symmetries between cotangent bundles of Braverman–Kazhdan spaces using reflection functors for quiver varieties.
